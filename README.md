# Application cockpit

A local, single-user job-search tool: scans company career-page APIs on a schedule, gates each posting against your declared languages and JD relevance before spending a token on it, generates a tailored CV and cover letter with deterministic fact-checking against your master CV, and renders a designed PDF. Nothing here ever submits an application on its own — every document is a draft you review and send yourself.

Full docs, screenshots, and setup instructions are coming. In the meantime, `config.yaml.example` and `sample-profile/` are enough to get a first scan and a sample CV rendering on a clean clone.
